# Credits for the pictures

The pictures in this folder are screenshots of yoyomi showing two works:

- [Pepper&Carrot](https://www.peppercarrot.com/) by David Revoy, licensed
  under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The pages
  are scaled to fit the reader's window and otherwise unchanged.
- The [Standard Ebooks](https://github.com/standardebooks/lewis-carroll_alices-adventures-in-wonderland_john-tenniel)
  edition of Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll, illustrated
  by John Tenniel, which is in the public domain.
